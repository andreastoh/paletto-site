Every photo has a palette. Frame it.

Support

Paletto reads the colours of a photo and sets it in a gallery-style matte with a swatch strip, ready to share or print. It's an iPhone and iPad app that works entirely on your device.

Restoring Paletto Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. On a new device, open Paletto, tap the crown, then Restore Purchase.

The widget shows an old palette

The widget shows the palette from the last frame you saved or shared. Save a frame, and it updates within a few seconds.

Pro frames look locked in the share sheet

The share extension can't make purchases. Buy or restore Pro in the Paletto app once, and the share sheet unlocks too.

Saving to Photos doesn't work

Paletto needs permission to add photos. Go to Settings → Apps → Paletto → Photos and allow it to add photos.

Caption ideas are missing

Caption ideas use Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported device running iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Something else?

Open an issue and describe what happened, with your device and iOS version. You'll get a reply there.
